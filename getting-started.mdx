SEC Football 2026 requires no login, no account, and no setup. Open the app, pick your team, and every game on your schedule is right in front of you — kickoff times, TV networks, live countdowns, and ticket links included.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App">
    Navigate to the SEC Football 2026 web app in your browser. The app works in any modern mobile or desktop browser — no installation required. You'll land directly on the Schedule tab and can start exploring right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Team">
    On the Schedule tab, locate the team picker at the top of the screen. Select your SEC team from the dropdown — all 16 teams are available, from Alabama through Vanderbilt. The app immediately loads your team's full 13-game regular season schedule and applies your school's colors throughout the interface.

    <Tip>
      Not sure which team to pick first? Start with your favorite and use the **Change Team** button later to browse any other team's schedule.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Your Schedule">
    Each game card on the Schedule tab displays everything you need to plan your gameday:

    - **Week number** and **date**
    - **Opponent** and whether the game is **home or away**
    - Whether it's a **conference game**
    - **Kickoff time** converted to the home venue's local time zone
    - **TV / streaming network**
    - **Result status** (shown as TBD until the game has been played)
    - **Live countdown** to kickoff

    Tap any game card to expand ticket links and additional details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Standings & Championship">
    Tap the **Standings** tab to see where all 16 SEC teams currently rank in the conference. Standings update throughout the season as results are reported.

    Tap the **Champion** tab to view full SEC Championship Game details — including the date, venue, broadcast information, and a history of recent conference champions — so you know exactly what your team is playing for.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching Teams

Want to check out a rival's schedule? Tap the **Change Team** button at the top of the Schedule tab. This returns you to the team picker so you can select any of the other 15 SEC teams and instantly view their full schedule. Your previous team selection is remembered if you navigate away and come back.

## No Account Required

<Note>
  SEC Football 2026 is completely free to use with no account, login, or registration required. Just open the app and start tracking the season — your privacy is never asked for in exchange for schedule data.
</Note>

## Device Support

SEC Football 2026 works across all your devices:

- **iPhone & iPad** — Fully optimized for Safari and other iOS browsers.
- **Android** — Works in Chrome, Firefox, and all major Android browsers.
- **Desktop** — Use it in any modern desktop browser on Mac or Windows.

<Info>
  On iPhone, you can add SEC Football 2026 to your Home Screen for a native app-like experience. In Safari, tap the **Share** icon and select **Add to Home Screen**. The app will open full-screen with no browser chrome, just like a native app.
</Info>
