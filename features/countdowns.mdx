SEC Football 2026 includes two types of live countdown timers — one at the top of every page counting down to the season opener, and one on each individual game card counting down to that game's kickoff. Both timers update every second in real time.

## Season Opener Countdown

A sticky header bar sits at the top of the app on every screen. It always counts down to the **season opener: Oklahoma vs. UTEP on September 4, 2026 at 7:00 PM CT**.

The countdown displays four units:

- **Days**
- **Hours**
- **Minutes**
- **Seconds**

This timer gives every fan a shared anchor to the start of the 2026 season, no matter which team's schedule or tab you're currently viewing.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the app or add it to your Home Screen for quick access to the countdown on game days — no need to search for it each week.
</Tip>

## Per-Game Countdown Timers

Each game card in your team's schedule includes its own dedicated countdown section. What the countdown shows depends on whether kickoff time has been announced:

**When kickoff time is confirmed**
The countdown shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds until that specific kickoff. The time displayed is always in the home venue's local time zone — see [Time Zone Display](#time-zone-display) below for details.

**When kickoff time is TBD**
The countdown shows the number of days remaining until the game date. The note *"Kickoff time TBD"* appears below the day count so you know an exact time hasn't been set yet.

**After the game**
Once a game has been played, the per-game countdown resets to **00:00:00:00** or shows a **Game Day** label, indicating the game has already occurred.

## Time Zone Display

Kickoff times in the app are shown in the **home venue's local time zone** — not your device's local time. Here's how SEC home venues break down by time zone:

**Eastern Time (ET)**
Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, South Carolina, Tennessee

**Central Time (CT)**
Alabama, Arkansas, Auburn, LSU, Mississippi State, Missouri, Oklahoma, Ole Miss, Texas, Texas A&M, Vanderbilt

<Note>
  If a kickoff shows **4:00 PM ET**, that time reflects the clock at the stadium — it may be a different hour where you are. Check your local time zone conversion before making travel or watch party plans.
</Note>

When your team plays an away game, the time shown is the home venue's local time. For example, if Georgia travels to Alabama, the kickoff is displayed in CT (Alabama's time zone), not ET.
