The Standings tab displays a ranked table of all 16 SEC teams and their season records. Use it to track the conference race throughout the year — from opening weekend all the way to the SEC Championship Game.

<Note>
  The 2026 standings start with all teams at 0-0. Records update throughout the season as game results are reported.
</Note>

## Reading the Standings Table

The standings table includes the following columns for each team:

| Column | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **#** | The team's current rank. Starts in preseason order and updates during the season as records change. |
| **Team** | The school name, accompanied by a color dot in that school's official primary color. |
| **W** | Total wins across all games (conference and non-conference). |
| **L** | Total losses across all games (conference and non-conference). |
| **Conf W** | Wins in SEC conference games only. |
| **Conf L** | Losses in SEC conference games only. |
| **Schedule** | A button that jumps you directly to that team's full schedule view. |

## Preseason vs. In-Season

At the start of the season, every team shows a **0-0** overall record and a **0-0** conference record — no games have been played yet. As the season progresses and results come in, the W, L, Conf W, and Conf L columns update to reflect each team's current standing.

Rankings in the **#** column begin in a fixed preseason order. They adjust during the season to reflect each team's conference record and overall performance.

## Navigating to a Team Schedule

Click the **Schedule** button next to any team in the standings table to load that team's full 13-game schedule. The app applies that school's official colors to the interface, so you can instantly check game times, results, and upcoming matchups — great for scouting a rival before a big game.

To return to the standings, use your browser's back button or tap the Standings tab in the navigation bar.
