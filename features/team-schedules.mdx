The Schedule tab is the heart of SEC Football 2026. Each of the 16 SEC teams has a full 13-game schedule displayed as individual game cards — giving you everything you need to follow your team from the season opener through rivalry week.

## Selecting Your Team

Use the team picker dropdown to choose from all 16 SEC teams. Once you select a team, the app loads that team's full schedule and applies your school's official colors to the interface — from the header accent to conference game highlights.

<Tip>
  Your selected team persists while you browse the app. Use the **Change Team** button (see [Switching Teams](#switching-teams)) any time you want to view a different program.
</Tip>

## Reading a Game Card

Each game in the schedule appears as its own card. Here's what every element on a game card means:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Week Number & Home/Away Label">
    The top line of each card shows the week number and whether your team is playing at home or on the road — for example, **Week 1 · HOME** or **Week 3 · AWAY**. Use this to plan travel for away games at a glance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CONF Badge">
    A **CONF** badge appears on any game that counts toward the SEC conference record. Games without this badge are non-conference matchups. See [Conference vs. Non-Conference Games](#conference-vs-non-conference-games) for more on how these are visually distinguished.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Game Date">
    The full calendar date of the game is displayed on every card (for example, *Saturday, September 5, 2026*). Dates are finalized before the season begins.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Matchup Display">
    Your team is always listed first. Home games use **vs** (e.g., *Alabama vs. Tennessee*) and away games use **at** (e.g., *Alabama at Georgia*). Both team names link to their respective schedule views.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Kickoff Time">
    Kickoff times are shown in the **home venue's local time zone** — either ET or CT depending on the stadium's location. If the kickoff hasn't been announced yet, the card shows **Kickoff TBD** instead of a time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="TV / Stream Network">
    The broadcast or streaming network for the game is listed on the card (e.g., CBS, ABC, FOX, ESPN). If the network hasn't been announced yet, the field shows **TBD**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Result Badge">
    Before a game is played, the result badge shows **TBD**. After the game concludes, it updates to **W** (win) or **L** (loss) based on the outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Per-Game Countdown Timer">
    Each game card includes a live countdown to kickoff, displaying days, hours, minutes, and seconds. If kickoff time is TBD, the countdown shows days remaining until the game date. See the [Countdowns](/features/countdowns) page for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Find Tickets Button">
    The **Find Tickets** button at the bottom of each game card opens a SeatGeek search pre-loaded with your matchup. See the [Tickets](/features/tickets) page for more information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Bye Weeks

When your team has a week off, a special **bye week card** appears in the schedule in place of a game card. The bye week card displays the week number and a "Bye Week" label. It does not include a matchup, kickoff time, TV network, countdown, or ticket button — there's simply no game to track that week.

<Note>
  Every SEC team has exactly one bye week during the regular season. Use your team's bye week card to quickly identify which week has no game.
</Note>

## Conference vs. Non-Conference Games

Conference games are easy to spot — they display a **left border accent** in your team's official school color. This visual highlight helps you quickly identify the games that affect the SEC standings.

Non-conference games appear as standard cards without the colored border accent. These games still count toward your team's overall win-loss record but do not affect conference standings.

## Switching Teams

Tap the **Change Team** button at the top of the schedule view to return to the team picker dropdown. Selecting a new team reloads the schedule and updates the interface colors to match the newly selected school.
