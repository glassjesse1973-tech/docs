## Who Plays in the Championship?

The SEC Championship Game features the top two teams by conference record at the conclusion of the regular season. The matchup is listed as **TBD** in the app until the regular season ends and the finalists are determined.

Once the finalists are set, the Championship tab updates with the two competing teams, their records, and ticket links through SeatGeek.
