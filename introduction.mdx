SEC Football 2026 is your all-in-one companion for the entire Southeastern Conference season. The app covers all 16 SEC member teams across the full 2026 schedule — from the season opener in September through the Conference Championship in December. Whether you want to check your team's upcoming matchups, track conference standings, or count down the minutes to kickoff, SEC Football 2026 keeps every stat, schedule, and championship detail in one place. Built for SEC football fans who want fast, accurate information without digging through multiple sites.

## Navigate the App

The app is organized into three main tabs so you can get to the information you need in one tap:

- **Schedule** — Your home base. Select any SEC team from the dropdown to load its full 13-game schedule, including kickoff countdowns, TV networks, and ticket links.
- **Standings** — See how all 16 SEC teams rank in the conference standings. Standings update automatically as the season progresses.
- **Champion** — Dive into the SEC Championship Game. Find the date, venue, broadcast details, and a year-by-year history of recent conference champions.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the app on your phone's home screen so you can check scores, countdowns, and standings with a single tap on gameday.
</Tip>
