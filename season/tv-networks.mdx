## When Will My Game's TV Info Be Available?

The SEC and its broadcast partners typically announce kickoff times and TV assignments on a rolling basis — often **6–12 days before the game**. Until the official announcement is made, game cards in the app will show **TBD** for both time and TV network. Check back closer to game day to see the confirmed details.

<Note>
  TV assignments and streaming rights may change. Always verify with your TV provider or the SEC's official communications closer to game day.
</Note>

## Streaming Options

If you can't get to a TV, games on CBS, ABC, and FOX are typically available through their respective streaming platforms:

- **CBS** → [Paramount+](https://www.paramountplus.com) (with eligible TV subscription or standalone plan)
- **ABC** → ESPN+ / ABC app (with eligible TV provider login)
- **FOX** → FOX Sports app (with eligible TV provider login)

Streaming availability depends on your provider and region. Check each platform's blackout and regional availability policies to confirm access before game day.
