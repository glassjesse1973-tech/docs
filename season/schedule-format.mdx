The 2026 SEC regular season runs from September 4 through November 28, with each of the 16 teams playing a 12-game schedule that includes 8–9 conference games, 3–4 non-conference games, and one bye week.

## Season Timeline

| Milestone | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Season opener | September 4, 2026 (Oklahoma vs. UTEP) |
| Regular season games begin | September 5, 2026 |
| Regular season finale | November 28, 2026 |
| SEC Championship Game | December 5, 2026 |

## Regular Season Format

Every team in the SEC plays the same number of games under the same structure:

- **12 games per team** across the regular season
- **1 bye week per team** — the exact week varies by team
- **8–9 conference games** — the precise count varies slightly depending on each team's schedule
- **3–4 non-conference games** — scheduled against opponents outside the SEC
- **Home and away games rotate** based on the conference schedule cycle

## Conference vs. Non-Conference Games

The app distinguishes between conference and non-conference matchups so you always know what's at stake.

- **Conference games** (SEC vs. SEC matchups) are marked with a **CONF** badge and displayed with a colored left-border accent on the game card.
- **Non-conference games** do not display the CONF badge or the accent border.
- **Conference game results** are what determine your team's standing in the SEC and whether they qualify for the SEC Championship Game.

## Bye Weeks

Each team has exactly one bye week during the 2026 season. No game is played during your team's bye week. The app shows a greyed-out, dashed-border **BYE WEEK** card in your schedule so you can follow the week-by-week flow of the season without losing track of where you are in the calendar.

## Result Tracking

The app tracks every game result as the season unfolds:

- **Before a game is played**, the result badge shows **TBD**.
- **After a game is played**, the badge updates to **W** (Win) or **L** (Loss).

The app updates throughout the season, so check back after each week's games to see your team's latest results.

## Home vs. Away

Each game card clearly shows whether your team is playing at home or on the road:

- Cards display **HOME** or **AWAY** to tell you the location at a glance.
- The label **"vs"** means your team is the home side. The label **"at"** means your team is playing away.
