## Mercedes-Benz Stadium

Mercedes-Benz Stadium is the home of the Atlanta Falcons (NFL) and Atlanta United FC (MLS), and has hosted the SEC Championship Game since 2017. The domed venue features a retractable roof and seats approximately 71,000 fans for football — making it one of the premier college football venues in the country.

## Qualification

The SEC Championship Game features the top qualified teams from the conference, determined by regular season conference record. Once the regular season wraps up on November 28, the finalists are set. Head to the **Championship** tab in the app to see the finalists once they are confirmed at the end of the regular season.
