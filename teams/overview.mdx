The 2026 SEC football season features 16 member programs competing across two divisions. SEC Football 2026 covers every team's full 13-game schedule.

## The Divisions

The SEC is organized into East and West divisions. The top qualifier(s) from each division meet in the **SEC Championship Game on December 5, 2026**.

## Finding a Team's Schedule

You have two ways to navigate to any team's schedule:

1. **Use the team picker on the Schedule tab** — open the Schedule tab and select any of the 16 programs from the team picker to load that team's full game-by-game schedule.
2. **Use the Standings tab** — visit the Standings tab and click the **Schedule** button next to any team to jump directly to that team's schedule view.

## Team Theming

When you select a team, the app applies that school's official colors throughout the interface. Header gradients, tab indicators, countdown numbers, and card accents all change to reflect your team's colors.
